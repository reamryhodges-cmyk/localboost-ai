We may use your information to:

  • Manage subscriptions, usage limits and account access.
  • Save your business profile and preferences.
  • Connect supported third-party advertising accounts when you ask us to do so.

  • Maintain, troubleshoot and improve LocalBoost AI.
  • Meet our legal, tax and regulatory obligations.

4. Our lawful bases for processing

Under UK data protection law, we must have a lawful basis for processing personal information.

Depending on the circumstances, our lawful bases may include:

  • Contract: where processing is necessary to provide a service you have requested or to take steps before entering into a contract with you.
  • Legitimate interests: where necessary for the legitimate operation, security and improvement of LocalBoost AI, provided those interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal obligation: where we need to process information to comply with applicable law.
  • Consent: where we specifically ask for and rely upon your consent.

5. Artificial intelligence

LocalBoost AI uses artificial intelligence services to help generate marketing materials based on information supplied by users.

Information entered into AI-generation features may be transmitted to technology providers that process the information on our behalf in order to generate the requested output.

You should not enter confidential personal information, passwords, payment-card information, special-category personal data, or information you do not have permission to use into AI prompts.

AI-generated material may contain errors. Users should review generated content before publishing or relying on it.

We only use connected-account information for the functionality you request and in accordance with the permissions granted through the relevant provider.
