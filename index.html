Frequently asked questions

Do I need marketing experience?

No. LocalBoost is designed to make creating business marketing content straightforward.

Does LocalBoost publish automatically?

You can review generated content before deciding where and how to use it. You remain responsible for checking and approving content before publication.

How does AI fit into LocalBoost?

AI powers content-generation features such as drafting marketing copy and ideas. The business and service are called LocalBoost; AI is a feature, not a replacement for your judgement.

How are payments handled?

Paid subscriptions use secure Stripe checkout. LocalBoost does not need to store your full payment-card details on its own systems.
